# Privacy Notice

NovaCommerce uses account, order, delivery, and support information to provide services, prevent fraud, process payments, and improve the customer experience. We limit access to personnel and service providers who need the information for these purposes.

## Information choices

Customers may request access to or correction of personal information through support. Marketing preferences can be changed using the unsubscribe control in a message or the account settings where available.

## Retention and protection

We retain records for operational, legal, tax, and fraud-prevention needs. We use access controls and security monitoring, but no online service can promise that every transmission is risk-free.

## Support conversations

Support does not need passwords, full payment-card numbers, authentication tokens, or private keys. Share only the order details needed to investigate a request.
