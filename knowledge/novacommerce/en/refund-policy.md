# Refund Policy

After an eligible return is received and approved, NovaCommerce issues the refund to the original payment method. Inspection normally completes within five business days of receipt.

## Timing

The refund instruction is sent after approval. Card issuers commonly display the credit within three to ten business days. The display time is controlled by the issuer, not NovaCommerce. Shipping charges are refundable only when the return is caused by our error, a defect, or a damaged delivery.

## Partial refunds

If a returned item is incomplete or shows use beyond normal inspection, support may offer a partial refund or return the item to the customer. We explain any deduction before finalizing it.

## Failed payments and cancellations

An authorization that is cancelled can remain visible temporarily as a pending hold. A completed charge is refunded under the original payment method rules. Live order and payment status must be checked through the Commerce tools or support system, not inferred from this policy.
