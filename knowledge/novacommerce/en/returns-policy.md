# Returns Policy

Most new, unused NovaCommerce products may be returned within 30 calendar days of delivery. Items must include the accessories, manuals, and packaging supplied with the order and must be in resalable condition.

## Exclusions

Opened software activation codes, personalized products, gift cards, and products marked final sale are not returnable unless they arrive damaged or defective. Hygiene-sensitive products cannot be returned after opening unless required by law.

## Starting a return

Use the order's return request flow or contact support with the order reference. Support provides return instructions and, when eligible, a prepaid label. Pack each item safely and keep the carrier receipt until the return is resolved.

## Inspection

NovaCommerce inspects returned items after receipt. A return can be rejected or a deduction can apply when an item is used, incomplete, or damaged beyond normal inspection. The refund policy describes timing and payment method.
