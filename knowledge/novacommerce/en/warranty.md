# Warranty

NovaCommerce-branded electronics include a limited one-year warranty against defects in materials and workmanship from the delivery date. The manufacturer warranty applies when a product listing identifies a different coverage period.

## Coverage

Warranty service covers repair, replacement, or another remedy selected under the applicable warranty terms. It does not cover accidental damage, liquid damage, normal cosmetic wear, lost items, unauthorized modification, or damage caused by unsupported accessories.

## Service request

Contact support with the order reference, product model, and a description of the problem. Do not include passwords, full payment-card numbers, or authentication tokens. Support may request troubleshooting steps and a safe shipping method.

## Returns are separate

Warranty service is not the same as the 30-day return window. A product outside the return window can still qualify for warranty review; eligibility is determined from the product and purchase records.
