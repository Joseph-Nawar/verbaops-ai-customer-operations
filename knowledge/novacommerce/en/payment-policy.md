# Payment Policy

NovaCommerce accepts the payment methods displayed at checkout, which may include major credit and debit cards, approved digital wallets, and promotional credit. Availability can vary by country, cart, and risk review.

## Authorization and capture

The payment provider may authorize a purchase before the order is dispatched. We capture payment for the items fulfilled under the order. A pending authorization is not proof that a completed charge exists; current payment state comes from the Commerce system.

## Security checks

For account protection, a payment may require an address or issuer verification step. NovaCommerce does not ask customers to disclose a password, one-time code, full card number, or security code in chat or email.

## Currency and disputes

The checkout total identifies the transaction currency, taxes, delivery charge, and discounts. Contact support first for a billing correction. Customers may also use their card issuer's dispute process under that issuer's rules.
