# Shipping Policy

NovaCommerce ships stocked products to addresses in the contiguous United States. The order confirmation shows the destination, delivery method, shipping charge, and estimated delivery window before payment is submitted.

## Delivery methods

Standard delivery normally arrives in three to five business days after dispatch. Express delivery normally arrives in one to two business days after dispatch. Orders placed after the daily fulfillment cutoff are processed on the next business day. Alaska, Hawaii, and remote locations may have different availability and estimates shown at checkout.

## Tracking and delays

We send a tracking link when a carrier accepts the parcel. Carrier scans can take one business day to appear. Weather, carrier disruption, incorrect address details, and inventory splits can delay delivery. Support can investigate a parcel after the carrier's stated delivery date has passed.

## Address changes

Contact support promptly if an address is wrong. We can request a correction before dispatch, but cannot guarantee a change after the parcel enters carrier possession. Never send payment credentials in a support message.
