# Accessories Guide

## Chargers and cables

Match a charger to the device's supported power profile. A higher wattage charger is not automatically faster or safer. Use certified cables in good condition and replace cables with exposed conductors.

## Cases and protection

Select a case for the exact model, not only the product family. Screen protectors and cases reduce some everyday wear but do not guarantee protection from drops or liquid.

## Compatibility checks

Review the product specifications for connector, dimensions, operating system, and included parts. Ask support if a compatibility question cannot be answered from the listing.
