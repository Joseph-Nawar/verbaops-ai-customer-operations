# Laptops Guide

## Choosing a laptop

For office work, prioritize keyboard comfort, memory, battery life, and warranty support. For creative or technical workloads, compare processor class, graphics capability, memory expandability, storage, display resolution, and thermal design.

## Setup and care

Install system and security updates, use a padded sleeve during transport, and keep ventilation clear. Back up important files before repair, reset, or return. Do not open a device while it is covered by a service process unless instructed by the manufacturer.

## Accessories

Check connector type, power requirements, and operating-system support before selecting a dock, monitor, charger, or replacement adapter.
