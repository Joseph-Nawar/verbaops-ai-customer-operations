# Phones and Tablets Guide

## Choosing a device

Compare operating system, screen size, storage, battery capacity, camera features, cellular compatibility, and included accessories. Confirm the model and storage option on the product page before ordering.

## Setup

Charge the device, connect it to a trusted network, install system updates, and use a unique account password. Transfer data through the manufacturer's supported migration flow and enable device locking and account recovery.

## Compatibility

Cases, chargers, cellular bands, and operating-system features vary by model. A USB-C connector does not guarantee identical charging speed or display support. Check the model-specific specifications before buying an accessory.
