# Payments FAQ

## Why is a card charge pending?

A pending authorization may appear before a payment is captured. The live payment record determines whether a charge completed.

## Can I use two payment methods?

Only combinations displayed by checkout are supported. Do not send card numbers or security codes to support.

## What if payment fails?

Confirm the billing details with the issuer and try an available method shown at checkout. Support can investigate an order payment record.
