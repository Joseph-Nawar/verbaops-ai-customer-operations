# Returns FAQ

## How long do I have to return an item?

Most new, unused products can be returned within 30 calendar days of delivery, subject to exclusions and inspection.

## Is a return label free?

Eligible returns receive instructions and, where applicable, a prepaid label. The return flow shows the option for the specific order.

## When is the refund sent?

After receipt and approval, NovaCommerce sends the refund to the original payment method. See the refund policy for typical issuer timing.
