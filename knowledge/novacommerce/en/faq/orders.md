# Orders FAQ

## How do I check an order?

Use the order-status support flow or contact support with the order reference. The live order record, not this FAQ, determines current status.

## Can I change an order?

Contact support immediately. An order can be changed only before the relevant fulfillment step and availability is not guaranteed.

## Why did an order split?

Items can ship separately when they are stocked at different locations or use different delivery methods. Each parcel receives its own tracking information.
