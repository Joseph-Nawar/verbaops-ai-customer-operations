# Warranty FAQ

## How long is the warranty?

NovaCommerce-branded electronics generally have a limited one-year warranty from delivery. A listing or manufacturer term can provide different coverage.

## Does accidental damage qualify?

Accidental, liquid, unauthorized-modification, and normal-wear damage are generally excluded from the limited warranty.

## What should I send support?

Provide the order reference, model, and a description of the fault. Never provide passwords, full card numbers, one-time codes, or tokens.
