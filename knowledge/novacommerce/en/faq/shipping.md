# Shipping FAQ

## When will my parcel arrive?

The order confirmation and live shipment record provide the current estimate. Standard delivery normally takes three to five business days after dispatch; carrier delays can change an estimate.

## Tracking has not updated

Carrier scans can take one business day after handoff. If the stated delivery date has passed, contact support for an investigation.

## Can I collect an order?

Only locations and orders that explicitly offer pickup are eligible. The available pickup slot must be selected during checkout.
